HTTP CACHE DIAGNOSIS FROM ONE URL

cachewhy

A 404 can remain fresh for 31 days. See the header that makes it happen.
$ cachewhy /missing.js
404 warning: browser may reuse this response for 31 days
Cache-Control: public, max-age=2678400
$ cachewhy '/missing.js?fixed=1'
Browser: DO NOT STORE no-store forbids storage
